Diwali 2025

A Celebration of Lights, Melodies, and Joy: The Diwali Fest Extravaganza

Transforming Building K3 into a vibrant festive hub of music, joy, and community.

Season

Diwali 2025

Venue

1st Floor, Building K3

Highlights

Music, Festive Stalls & Pyramid Game

Festive Archive

Laughter, Energy, and Traditional Warmth

When the festive season arrived on campus, the first floor of Building K3 transformed into a vibrant hub of celebration, laughter, and high-energy entertainment during the annual Diwali Fest. Stepping away from the standard academic routine, students were welcomed into a brilliantly decorated, immersive space designed to spark joy and foster a deep sense of community. The fest successfully blended the warmth of traditional celebrations with modern interactive elements, creating an unforgettable experience that brought together crowds of eager participants from all corners of the campus.

At the heart of the fest was an irresistible blend of engaging music and playful competition. The air was filled with lively, uplifting tunes playing continuously over speakers, setting a cheerful and festive mood that immediately drew people in. Students couldn't resist tapping their feet, dancing, and singing along, turning the hallway into a lively dance floor where everyone felt included. Adding to the excitement were the crowd-favorite activity corners: two bustling gift shop stalls filled with festive goodies and quirky items, alongside the wildly popular and competitive "Break the Glass Pyramid" game. This interactive game became a massive hit, drawing cheers, friendly rivalry, and endless laughter as participants tested their precision and luck.

Fest Attractions

Continuous Festive Tunes

Uplifting holiday tracks playing through the floor, turning the hallway into an impromptu dance floor for students and faculty.

Festive Gift Stalls

Two curated shopping counters featuring festive goodies, handmade crafts, quirky accessories, and festive tokens.

Break the Glass Pyramid

The interactive crowd favorite testing precision and aim, drawing nonstop cheers and friendly rivalry throughout the day.

Immersive Decor

Handcrafted rangolis, vibrant fairy lights, and traditional ornaments illuminating the corridor with celebration spirit.
