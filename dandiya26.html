When theatrical storytelling and high-tempo Bollywood beats illuminated K3 Block.

Official Event Report

An Unforgettable Afternoon of Festive Energy

Fusion Cultural Society lit up K3 Block ASB, First Floor, on Friday, 16th October 2026 with Bollywood Dandiya, a celebration of music, color and non-stop energy that brought students together for an unforgettable experience. The event began with a beautiful skit by the Dramatics Club, which instantly captured everyone's attention. With expressive acting, great stage presence and a story that kept the audience hooked, the performance set the perfect tone for the festivities ahead. The applause at the end said it all, and it got the crowd warmed up and ready to dance.

Then the DJ took over with a playlist of fun, foot-tapping Bollywood favorites, and the floor filled with clacking sticks, swirling dupattas and students having the time of their lives. Our sponsor, Multiworkwala, supported the event with exciting hampers for the Best Dancers, Best Costume and the DJ. Congratulations to all the winners, and a big thank you to the Dramatics Club, the DJ, Multiworkwala and every student who danced with us. Stay tuned for more from Fusion Cultural Society!
